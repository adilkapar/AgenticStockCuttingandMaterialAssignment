The fundamental challenge is that raw materials must be procured 4-8 weeks before customer orders are confirmed, because supplier lead times exceed order confirmation horizons. Each coil is characterized by 13 physical parameters (product group, quality, thickness, width, surface group, coating amount, outer/inner diameter, weight, roughness, passivation, protective oil amount, and origin) that must match customer order specifications. Using an Agentic AI architecture with SAP Joule as orchestrator, specialized Order and Stock agents, and an MCP-based optimization server powered by OR-Tools, the system automates feasibility matching, optimal material assignment, and cutting pattern generation — achieving 2-4% trim loss versus 8-15% with manual planning — then writes results directly back to SAP S/4HANA as reservations and production orders. Since each steel service center has different product mixes, machine capabilities, and business priorities, the system is configurable through objective weights and constraint parameters."

SLIDE 3 — Challenges, Solution, Benefits, Internal Comments Same title bar and divider as slide 2. 2×2 quad layout — each quadrant has a teal uppercase bold section header followed by body text:

TOP-LEFT — "CHALLENGES AND BUSINESS DRIVERS": "Steel Service Centers face a structurally hard optimization problem: raw material coils must be purchased against forecasts weeks before customer orders are confirmed, because supplier lead times (4-8 weeks) exceed order horizons (1-2 weeks). Each coil has 13 physical parameters that must match order specifications almost exactly — making procurement nearly order-specific. Manual planning relies on individual expertise, takes hours, produces 8-15% trim waste, and breaks daily due to order changes, supply disruptions, and forecast errors. Standard SAP PP/MRP does not solve the combinatorial cutting pattern and material assignment optimization natively. The lack of integrated optimization leads to excessive scrap, tardy deliveries, idle stock, and over-reliance on key planning personnel."

TOP-RIGHT — "SOLUTION": "An Agentic AI system built on SAP BTP combining: (1) SAP Joule Orchestrator — natural language interface embedded in SAP Fiori, using LLM reasoning (ReAct pattern) to decompose planning requests, delegate to domain agents, invoke optimization tools, explain trade-offs, and write confirmed plans back to SAP. (2) Order Agent — reads SAP S/4HANA SD sales orders and forecasts, evaluates feasibility, tracks fulfillment, identifies urgent and infeasible orders. (3) Stock Agent — reads SAP S/4HANA MM coil inventory (stock, in-transit, purchase orders), manages reservations, availability calculations, and idle coil detection. (4) MCP Optimization Server (Python/OR-Tools) — exposes 5 tools: feasibility network builder with connected component decomposition, 1D/2D cutting pattern solver, multi-objective MILP assignment optimizer, plan evaluator, and purchase recommender. (5) SAP Integration — reads via CDS views/OData, writes via BAPI (reservations MB21, production orders CO01), reacts via SAP Event Mesh."

BOTTOM-LEFT — "BENEFITS": "Trim loss reduction from 8-15% (manual) to 2-4% (optimized) — representing 50-75% waste reduction and significant raw material cost savings. Planning time reduction from hours to less than 15 seconds for a full planning run. Order fulfillment improvement to 90%+ with automatic identification of supply gaps. Idle stock reduction of 20-40% through proactive coil-to-order matching. For a typical operation of 500 tons/month at EUR 800/ton, this translates to EUR 28,000/month savings from trim reduction alone. Additional benefits: elimination of person-dependency in planning, full explainability of every decision, event-driven proactive re-planning, what-if scenario exploration, and every plan immediately actionable as SAP shop orders."

BOTTOM-RIGHT — "INTERNAL COMMENTS": "The system solves the fundamental contradiction we face every day: we buy steel before knowing what customers will order, and then we need to figure out the best way to cut it. Having an AI agent that can reason about 13 parameters across hundreds of coils and orders, explain WHY it made each decision, and then create the SAP transactions — that changes everything for our planning team." — Production Planning Manager, Steel Service Center
